Data Engineer interviews go a level deeper than analyst screens on SQL,
and add **data modeling**, **pipeline/orchestration**, and **systems**
questions. Expect hard SQL (window functions, dedup, slowly-changing
dimensions), plus "design a pipeline for X" discussions.

## What interviewers probe

- **Advanced SQL** — window functions, `QUALIFY`, deduplication, gaps-and-islands, incremental logic.
- **Data modeling** — star vs snowflake schemas, fact/dimension tables, normalization trade-offs, SCD types.
- **Pipelines & orchestration** — batch vs streaming, idempotency, backfills, partitioning, scheduling (Airflow/Dagster).
- **Systems & formats** — columnar formats (Parquet), partitioning/file sizing, warehouse vs lakehouse, cost.

- **[SQL: window functions](/interview/data-engineer/sql-window-functions)** — a runnable ranking challenge plus a concept check.
