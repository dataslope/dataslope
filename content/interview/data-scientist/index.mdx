Data Scientist interviews blend **statistics**, **modeling**, and
**experimentation** with coding and communication. Expect probability
and stats questions, a modeling case ("how would you predict X?"), an
A/B-test design discussion, and usually a Python/pandas or SQL screen.

## What interviewers probe

- **Statistics & probability** — distributions, hypothesis testing, p-values, confidence intervals, Bayes.
- **Modeling** — bias–variance trade-off, regularization, cross-validation, feature engineering, metric choice.
- **Experimentation** — A/B test design, power, sample size, novelty effects, pitfalls.
- **Coding** — pandas/NumPy data wrangling and SQL.
- **Communication** — explaining a model and its caveats to a non-technical stakeholder.
