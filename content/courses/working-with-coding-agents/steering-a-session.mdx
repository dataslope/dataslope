Most sessions need at least one correction. How you give it decides
whether the agent fixes the problem or apologizes and repeats it.

## Specific feedback beats "that's wrong"

{/*
- Say what is wrong, where, and what right looks like: "the total
  should exclude refunded orders; see the status column".
- Paste the actual error or the failing output rather than describing
  it.
- Interactive: MultipleChoice choosing between three corrections for
  the same recorded mistake.
*/}

## Interrupt early

{/*
- When the plan or the first edit goes in the wrong direction, stop it
  there. Every further step builds on the mistake and adds to the diff.
*/}

## Continue or start over

{/*
- A long session carries every failed attempt in its context (What the
  Agent Sees).
- Rule of thumb to write up: after two corrections on the same point,
  restart with a better request that includes what you learned.
*/}
